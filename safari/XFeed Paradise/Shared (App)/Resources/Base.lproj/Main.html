XFeed Paradise

Filters outrage-bait out of x.com and Google News in Safari, and keeps the posts worth reading.

Turn it on

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps → Safari → Extensions → XFeed Paradise.
    On iOS 17 and earlier: Settings → Safari → Extensions.
  3. Turn on Allow Extension.
  4. Under Permissions, set x.com, news.google.com and api.groq.com (plus any other AI provider you use) to Allow.

Or, in Safari on x.com: tap the page menu in the address bar → XFeed Paradise → Always Allow on This Website.

Then open x.com in Safari. Tap the round palm-tree button for filter settings, or the extension’s icon in Safari’s page menu.

You can turn on XFeed Paradise’s extension in Safari Extensions preferences.

XFeed Paradise’s extension is currently on. You can turn it off in Safari Extensions preferences.

XFeed Paradise’s extension is currently off. You can turn it on in Safari Extensions preferences.

After turning it on, allow x.com, news.google.com and api.groq.com (or choose Always Allow on Every Website).
