## Short Description (132 characters max)

## Detailed Description
Transform your social media experience with XFeed Paradise - an AI-powered extension that brings peace to your feed.

**How it works:**
XFeed Paradise scores posts and news items you encounter using local keyword rules. You can optionally bring your own key from Groq (free), OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Together or TypeSafe, or point it at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint such as a local Ollama server, for AI refinement. Content that's uplifting, enlightening, or genuinely informative gets highlighted. Outrage-bait, doom-scrolling fodder, and toxic content gets filtered out.

**Key Features:**

- Bring Your Own Key: Use the AI provider you already pay for, or Groq's free tier
- Fallback Provider: Optionally add a second provider that takes over when the first fails or hits a rate limit
- Customizable Threshold: Set your own vibe filter level from -100 to +100
- Multiple Filter Modes: Hide, dim, or collapse low-vibe content
- Visual Vibe Badges: See scores on tweets at a glance
- Works on X/Twitter and Google News
- Local feed history: Viewed items stay in your browser's IndexedDB; there is no community-feed upload
- Clear AI status: Test your provider connection and see when scoring falls back to another provider or to keywords

**Why XFeed Paradise?**

Social media algorithms optimize for engagement, not your wellbeing. Outrage and conflict drive clicks, but they also drive anxiety and polarization. XFeed Paradise flips the script - now YOUR feed optimizes for content that leaves you feeling better, not worse.

**Free to Use:**
- Works with local keyword scoring without an API key
- Add a free Groq API key for GPT-OSS 20B scoring (subject to Groq's rate limits), or a key from another supported provider
- When AI is enabled, item text is sent to the provider you choose for scoring; keys may sync with your signed-in Chrome profile

**Open Source:**
XFeed Paradise is fully open source. Review the code, suggest features, or contribute at:
https://github.com/opentoolshub/xfeedparadise
