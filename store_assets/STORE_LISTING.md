## Short Description (132 characters max)

## Detailed Description
Transform your social media experience with XFeed Paradise - an AI-powered extension that brings peace to your feed.

**How it works:**
XFeed Paradise scores posts and news items you encounter with AI out of the box: the built-in provider is free with a daily limit and needs no API key. Local keyword rules take over when AI is off or the limit is reached. You can also bring your own key from Groq (free), OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Together or TypeSafe, or point it at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint such as a local Ollama server, for AI refinement. Content that's uplifting, enlightening, or genuinely informative gets highlighted. Outrage-bait, doom-scrolling fodder, and toxic content gets filtered out.

**Key Features:**

- Built-in AI: Free AI scoring with no key, up to a daily limit
- Bring Your Own Key: Use the AI provider you already pay for, or Groq's free tier, for unlimited scoring
- Fallback Provider: Optionally add a second provider that takes over when the first fails or hits a rate limit
- Customizable Threshold: Set your own vibe filter level from -100 to +100
- Multiple Filter Modes: Hide, dim, or collapse low-vibe content
- Visual Vibe Badges: See scores on tweets at a glance
- Works on X/Twitter and Google News
- Local feed history: Viewed items stay in your browser's IndexedDB; there is no community-feed upload
- Clear AI status: Test your provider connection and see when scoring falls back to another provider or to keywords

**Why XFeed Paradise?**

Social media algorithms optimize for engagement, not your wellbeing. Outrage and conflict drive clicks, but they also drive anxiety and polarization. XFeed Paradise flips the script - now YOUR feed optimizes for content that leaves you feeling better, not worse.

**Free to Use:**
- Built-in AI scoring with no API key, up to a daily limit; local keyword scoring after that
- Add a free Groq API key for GPT-OSS 20B scoring (subject to Groq's rate limits), or a key from another supported provider
- When AI is enabled, item text is sent for scoring: with the built-in provider, to the XFeed Paradise scoring server, which forwards it to OpenAI or TypeSafe and does not keep it; otherwise to the provider you choose. Keys may sync with your signed-in Chrome profile
